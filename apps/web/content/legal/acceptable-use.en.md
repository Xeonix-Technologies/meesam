# Acceptable Use Policy

<!-- source: docs/03-legal/MSM — Acceptable Use Policy v0.1 (draft, 2026-11-12).pdf; dated Nov 12, 2026; the "Notes for review" section is internal and is not published -->

## 1. Scope

**1.1** This policy applies to every Account and user of Meesam. It forms part of the Terms of Use, and words defined there have the same meaning here.

## 2. What you must not do

**2.1** Records and documents. You must not:

enter a mark, or upload a document, that you have no right or authority to enter; enter false or misleading data, or forge or alter a certificate, power of attorney or other document; claim ownership of, or an Appointment over, a mark that is not yours or your client's; upload content that is unlawful, or that infringes another person's rights.

**2.2** Accounts. You must not:

share log-in details or let one person use another's user profile; open an Account under a false identity or a licence that is not yours; be a user of more than one agency office account; use an Owner pass that was not issued to you.

**2.3** The Service. You must not:

copy, scrape or extract data from Meesam by automated means, except through the export functions; copy the agency directory or use it to send bulk or unsolicited messages; reverse engineer the Service, test its security without our written consent, or get around access controls; upload malware or interfere with the Service or other users; resell the Service or run it as a bureau for third parties, except an agency managing its own clients' marks; use the Service to build a competing product or to train an AI model.

**2.4** Conduct. You must not:

use message threads or quote requests to harass, mislead or spam; present Meesam, an Alert or a report as legal advice, as an official Registry document or as a Registry decision; use the Service where sanctions or export laws forbid it.

## 3. Rules for agencies and the directory

**3.1** An agency must hold, and keep current, the licence it was verified with. It must tell us at once if the licence lapses, is suspended or changes.

**3.2** An agency must have its client's authority before entering the client's marks, data and documents.

**3.3** Directory profiles and quotes must be accurate. An agency must not quote for work it is not licensed to do in that country.

**3.4** An agency must not use information from a quote request for any purpose except answering it.

## 4. What we may do

**4.1** If we reasonably believe this policy is breached, we may: ask you to put it right; remove or hide content; remove an agency from the directory; suspend a user or an Account; or close the Account under the Terms of Use.

**4.2** Where it is safe and lawful, we tell you first and give you a chance to respond. We may act at once where there is a risk to security, to another user or to the Service.

**4.3** We may report unlawful conduct to the authorities.

## 5. Reporting a problem

**5.1** To report a breach of this policy, a mark entered without authority, or content that infringes your rights, write to [abuse email] with the Record or page concerned and your reason. We review reports within [5 working days].
