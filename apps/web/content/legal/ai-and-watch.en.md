## 1. Where Meesam uses AI

**1.1** This notice forms part of the Terms of Use. It tells you where Meesam uses artificial intelligence and what the results can and cannot be relied on for.

**1.2** Meesam uses AI in four places: reading the documents you upload; reading Registry publications; comparing marks in the Watch; and the help service.

**1.3** AI results can be wrong. They support your decision and do not replace your own check or professional advice.

## 2. AI reading of your documents

**2.1** When you upload a document, AI reads it and proposes the data for the Record. You review, correct and accept the data before it is saved. You can also enter data by hand.

**2.2** Meesam flags data or dates that look inconsistent. These checks only warn and are not complete.

**2.3** Once you accept data, it is your data. Check it against the original document and the Registry.

## 3. How the Watch works

**3.1** For each Record with the Watch on, you select the Registries to watch and the classes to watch.

**3.2** Meesam downloads the publications of those Registries automatically and reads the newly published marks with AI. [A person on our staff checks AI-read publication data against the publication before the Watch relies on it. We may remove this step for a Registry once accuracy is proven or the Registry supplies the data directly.]

**3.3** The Watch compares each published mark with your mark during the Registry's opposition period. It compares words, images, words found inside logos, and how names sound across scripts, starting with Arabic and Latin.

**3.4** International (Madrid) designations are watched only where the Registry itself publishes them. [Data from WIPO is not used at this date.]

**3.5** The Registries covered are those on the "Registries available" line on the date you check.

**3.6** Each Account's marks are watched separately. Your Alerts and their status are not shown to other Accounts, except the owner and agencies linked to the same Record.

**3.7** The Watch does not alert you to your own filing of the same mark.

## 4. Alerts and risk bands

**4.1** An Alert shows the published mark, your mark, the Registry, the last day to oppose, and one of three risk bands: [High, Medium, Low].

**4.2** A risk band is a similarity score produced by software. It is not a legal assessment of likelihood of confusion and takes no account of facts outside the two marks, such as actual use, reputation or agreements.

**4.3** Each Registry has its own Alert. Alerts for the same mark at different Registries are not merged.

**4.4** Reminders follow an open Alert when it is created, 10 days before and 3 days before the last day to oppose.

**4.5** You can mark an Alert as relevant or not relevant. [This feedback tunes later Alerts.]

**4.6** [Target: Alerts are issued within 2 working days of the Registry's publication. This is a target, not a guarantee.]

## 5. Limits of the Watch

**5.1** The Watch can miss a similar mark, and it can alert you to a mark that is not a conflict. No Alert does not mean no conflict.

**5.2** The Watch covers only the Registries and classes you selected, only marks published while the Watch is on for that Record, and only what the Registry actually published.

**5.3** A Registry may publish late, correct or withdraw a publication, or change its format. The Watch may be delayed or incomplete as a result.

**5.4** The Watch does not check unpublished applications, unregistered marks, company names, domain names or use in the market.

**5.5** The last day to oppose shown on an Alert is calculated from the publication date and Meesam's profile of the Registry. Confirm it with the Registry or your agency before you rely on it.

**5.6** Meesam does not advise whether to oppose and does not file oppositions.

## 6. Retrospective conflict report

**6.1** When you load existing marks, Meesam may give you a report of marks already published that look similar to yours. [Period and Registries covered: to be stated on the report.]

**6.2** The report is produced by the same comparison software as the Watch and has the same limits. It is not a clearance search, a legal opinion or a full search of any register.

## 7. Dates, stages and reminders

**7.1** Stages and dates in a Record come from the data the Account holders entered or accepted, and from Meesam's profile of the Registry. They are not the Registry's official status.

**7.2** Where Meesam has not verified a Registry period, you enter the deadline yourself.

**7.3** A period ends at 23:59 in the Registry's local time on its last day. Meesam does not move a deadline that falls on a non-working day.

**7.4** Renewal reminders are sent when the renewal window opens and 6, 3 and 1 months before expiry. Reminders are a convenience and can fail to arrive.

## 8. AI-assisted help

**8.1** The help service uses AI to answer questions about using Meesam. It does not give legal advice and may be wrong about a Registry's rules.

**8.2** For a technical or payment issue you can reach a person at [support email].
